# Development Checklist

## Versioning Conventions

Version numbers follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`:

- **Major (`#.0.0`)** — A major redesign or site-wide architectural overhaul.
- **Minor (`x.#.0`)** — A planned feature release. **All features, content, layout, and design tasks completed for that release are listed under its `.0` version** (for example, `1.3.0` or `1.4.0`), not numbered as separate patches.
- **Patch / hotfix (`x.x.#`)** — A correction or small update **after the initial minor release**, recorded as `.1`, `.2`, and so on, in release order. Do not assign patch numbers to tasks that are part of the initial `.0` release.

Major and minor versions can have a custom release name chosen later; use `[INSERT NAME]` as the placeholder until named. Historical 1.1 and 1.2 checklist numbers are preserved as originally reconstructed; the corrected release grouping applies to 1.3 onward.
